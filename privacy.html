lenovo-5pro-backup privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-03.

  • The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. It can see and modify only the files and folders it created itself, and nothing else in the user's Drive.
  • No data is collected, sold or shared with third parties.
  • There are no analytics and no cookies.
  • The only user is the owner.
  • Access can be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
